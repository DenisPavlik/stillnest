Static shot. The camera remains completely still, holding the identical framing throughout. Flames curl and flicker behind the glass door of the wood stove, and the embers glow and pulse orange. The candle flames on the table flicker gently. A thin wisp of steam rises from the mug and from the kettle and curls slowly away. Beyond the tall windows, fine snow falls slowly between the pines. The pendant lamps, the furniture, the walls and the window frames remain still. Exposure, contrast and colour remain constant.
