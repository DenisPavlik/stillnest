Static shot. The camera remains completely still, holding the identical framing throughout. Flames curl and flicker behind the glass of the wood stove. The candle flames on the tray flicker gently. A thin wisp of steam rises from the mug and curls slowly away. Beyond the glass, steam rises slowly from the cedar tub and mist drifts between the tree ferns, which sway slightly. The paper lantern, the sofa, the bookshelves and the window frames remain still. Exposure, contrast and colour remain constant.
