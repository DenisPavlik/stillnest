Static shot. The camera remains completely still, holding the identical framing throughout. Beyond the glass, steam rises and drifts slowly from the geothermal vents on the mountainside and off the surface of the hot pool, which ripples gently. The lantern flame and the candle flames flicker gently, and flames curl behind the glass of the wood stove. The robes, the sofa, the lamp and the window frames remain still. Exposure, contrast and colour remain constant.
