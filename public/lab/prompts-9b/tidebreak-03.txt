Static shot. The camera remains completely still, holding the identical framing throughout. Beyond the glass, waves surge against the black rocks and white spray bursts upward and falls back. Raindrops run slowly down the window panes. The candle flames on the table flicker gently, and the fire glows and flickers in the stove. The pendant lamps, the dried flowers, the table, the chairs and the window frames remain still. Exposure, contrast and colour remain constant.
