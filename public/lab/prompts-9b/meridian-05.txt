Static shot. The camera remains completely still, holding the identical framing throughout. Water pours steadily from the spout in the concrete wall into the channel, and its surface ripples. Sunlight shimmers across the water of the channel and the pool. The leaves of the olive trees stir slightly in a warm breeze. The striped shadows, the walls, the table, the cushions and the cliffs remain still. Exposure, contrast and colour remain constant.
