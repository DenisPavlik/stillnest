Static shot. The camera remains completely still, holding the identical framing throughout. Flames curl and flicker behind the glass of the wood stove. The small candle flames on the counter flicker gently. A thin wisp of steam rises from the mug on the side table and curls slowly away. Beyond the glass, the fjord water ripples gently and the reflections of the lights shimmer on its surface. The lamps, the sofa, the walls and the window frames remain still. Exposure, contrast and colour remain constant.
