Static shot. The camera remains completely still, holding the identical framing throughout. Flames curl and flicker behind the glass of the wood stove. The candle flames on the table flicker gently. A thin wisp of steam rises from the cup and curls slowly away. Beyond the glass, clouds drift slowly across the dusk sky and the ferns outside sway slightly. The roof beams, the lamps, the sofa and the window frames remain still. Exposure, contrast and colour remain constant.
