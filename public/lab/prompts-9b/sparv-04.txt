Static shot. The camera remains completely still, holding the identical framing throughout. The green aurora ripples and shifts slowly across the night sky above the frozen lake. Flames flicker behind the glass of the wood stove. The candle flames on the table flicker gently. A thin wisp of steam rises from the mug and curls slowly away. The stars, the pine trees, the furniture, the lamps and the window frames remain still. Exposure, contrast and colour remain constant.
