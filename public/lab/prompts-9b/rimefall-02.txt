Static shot. The camera remains completely still, holding the identical framing throughout. Flames curl and flicker behind the glass of the wood stove. The candle flames on the table flicker gently. A thin wisp of steam rises from the teapot and the cup and curls slowly away. Beyond the glass, smoke drifts slowly from the chimney of the distant hut and clouds move slowly across the sky. The pendant lamps, the furniture, the skis and the window frames remain still. Exposure, contrast and colour remain constant.
