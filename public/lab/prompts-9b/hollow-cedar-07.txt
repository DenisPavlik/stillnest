Static shot. The camera remains completely still, holding the identical framing throughout. Steady rain falls beyond the open wall, streaking down through the bamboo. Rainwater drips from the edge of the roof, and raindrops ripple the surface of the wooden tub and the wet deck. The bamboo and the ferns sway slightly in the rain. The two candle flames flicker gently. The paper lanterns, the furniture, the walls and the screens remain still. Exposure, contrast and colour remain constant.
