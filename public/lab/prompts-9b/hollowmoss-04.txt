Static shot. The camera remains completely still, holding the identical framing throughout. Flames curl and flicker behind the glass of the wood stove. A thin wisp of steam rises from the enamel pot and the mug and curls slowly away. The candle flames flicker gently. Beyond the glass, morning mist drifts slowly across the still lake and between the trees. The bookshelves, the furniture, the lamp and the window frames remain still. Exposure, contrast and colour remain constant.
